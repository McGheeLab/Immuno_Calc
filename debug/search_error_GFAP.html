GFAP Primary Antibody Antibody Products from multiple suppliers

Antibodies are essential reagents for detecting specific proteins and biomarkers in applications including flow cytometry, Western blotting, IHC, ICC, immunofluorescence, and immunoprecipitation. The Biocompare database enables users to freely search a catalog of millions of antibodies from the most popular antibody suppliers. To help navigate this extensive list, use the provided filters. Narrow the search by filtering for any desired antibody type, application, species reactivity, host species, clonality, modifications, conjugate, isotype, and antibody company. Users can also filter for antibodies cited in the literature, associated with published figures, and have been independently reviewed by other researchers. Visit the supplier page for more antibody information, including target specificity, validation data, datasheets, and any potential cross-reactivities.
